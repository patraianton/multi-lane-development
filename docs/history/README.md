# history — sources, not norms

These files are the contracts and logs of the board's earlier shape (windows, the CTO window, the stream
window, ticket cutting by hand, the decisions log up to 2026-08-30). They are kept as the record of why
things are the way they are. The norms today are `docs/RULES.md`, `docs/BOARD.md`, `docs/FLEET.md` and
`docs/API.md`; the design is `docs/specs/2026-08-30-board-is-the-scheduler.md`.
